# C3 — Supplier permission request for AI browser carts

Prepared: 2026-11-14 under
[decision 0008](decisions/0008-ai-browser-only-with-supplier-permission.md).
The owner sends each request and keeps the replies. Nothing here has been sent
by the agent, and no supplier has been contacted.

## Before sending

- **Send to** the supplier's customer support, business sales or integration
  contact, or the café's own sales representative only with the café's
  permission.
- **Fill in** every bracketed part truthfully. Name the café only with its
  permission.
- **Ask for the reply in writing** (email is fine). A phone "yes" is not enough.

## Email draft

> **Subject:** Permission request: software-prepared carts on [Supplier]
>
> Hello [Supplier] team,
>
> I'm building Pantrack, inventory and purchasing software for small cafés and
> restaurants. [The café I'm working with orders / Cafés I work with order] from
> [Supplier], and before offering anything I'd like to check how you want
> software like ours to work with your website.
>
> What Pantrack would do:
>
> - After a manager reviews and approves an order in Pantrack, a browser
>   assistant opens [Supplier]'s website in the customer's own account and adds
>   exactly those items to the cart.
> - The manager signs in themselves. Pantrack never stores their password.
> - The manager reviews the cart and places the order themselves. Pantrack never
>   checks out or pays.
> - About [one cart per customer per week], at a normal browsing pace. No catalog
>   copying, price monitoring or scraping.
>
> My questions:
>
> 1. Do you offer an official ordering connection for business software, such as
>    an API, EDI or a punchout catalog? If so, we'd prefer to use it.
> 2. If not, would you give written permission for the limited use above? We're
>    happy to follow any conditions, such as times of day, request limits or
>    identifying our browser.
> 3. If you're not the right person for this, who is?
>
> We won't use automation on your website unless you confirm it's allowed.
>
> Thank you,
> [Your name]
> Pantrack
> [Contact email]
