# W4d photo waste switch and monthly cap — local evidence

Recorded: 2026-11-16. Branch: `workstream-c/photo-waste-w4d`, based on `main`
at `db13186`. Step W4d of the
[photo waste plan](WASTE_AND_MARGIN_ROADMAP.md#w4--photo-waste-suggestions),
following [decision 0010](decisions/0010-photo-waste-suggestions.md). **Local
evidence only, with the offline fake reader.** Adds migration `0052`; no other
schema-bearing change is pending.

## Changed behavior

- **Off by default; only the owner switches it**
  ([migration 0052](../drizzle/0052_waste_photo_settings.sql)):
  - `waste_photo_settings` stores the switch per company, with who changed it
    and when. While it is off, suggestion requests are refused (403
    `not_enabled`) before anything is read; replays of earlier requests still
    answer, at no cost.
  - `GET/POST /api/waste/suggestions/settings` use a new `waste-photo` route
    family that needs the owner's integrations permission, with the usual
    sign-in, same-site, company and audit handling.
- **$10 a month per company**
  ([service](../src/lib/d1-waste-suggestions.ts),
  [contract](../src/lib/waste-suggestion-contract.ts)):
  - The month is the UTC calendar month.
  - Claiming a request inserts it with a $0.05 reserve, but only if this
    month's total plus the reserve stays within $10. This happens in one
    statement, so simultaneous requests cannot spend past the cap. Otherwise
    the request is refused (429 `cap_reached`) without reading.
  - After reading, the reserve is replaced by the actual cost. A failed read
    keeps its reserve, since it may have been billed. W4e must keep each real
    request under the reserve.
- **What people see**
  ([`photo-waste-suggest.tsx`](../src/components/workspace/photo-waste-suggest.tsx)):
  - The owner sees a card above the panel: "Photo and note suggestions are
    off", with **Turn on** and a plain explanation of who can use it and the
    $10 OpenAI limit. Once on, it shows "$0.00 of $10.00 used this month" and
    **Turn off**.
  - Everyone else sees the panel only when it is on.
  - At the limit the panel says "This month's $10 limit for suggestions is
    reached. Use the waste form below; it starts again next month."
  - `GET /api/waste/items` now gives `photoSuggestions: {connected, enabled,
    limitReached}`; spending amounts are shown only to the owner.
  - Nothing appears unless a reader is configured on the server, which is
    still only the local fake.

## Schema review

- **SQL, snapshot and journal:** one table keyed by company, with a check that
  `enabled` is 0 or 1, generated by `pnpm db:generate` from
  [`schema.ts`](../src/db/schema.ts). Spending reuses `cost_micro_usd` from
  `0051`.
- **Fresh and populated databases:** `pnpm db:check` applied all 53
  migrations to fresh SQLite. `pnpm db:migrate:local` applied `0052` to the
  local development database.
- **Compatibility:** the change is additive. Without a row, suggestions are
  off.
- **Rollback / forward repair:** revert the code; the table can stay. Never
  edit this migration once applied.

## Verification

The complete local pipeline passed in this worktree on 2026-11-16 with the
changes staged: `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm test` (181 suites), `pnpm db:check`
(53 migrations), `pnpm build`, `pnpm db:migrate:local` and `pnpm test:local`.

- [`tests/waste-suggestions.mjs`](../tests/waste-suggestions.mjs) covers:
  - **off by default:** nothing is read or stored;
  - **spending:** usage for a new month, actual cost replacing the reserve, a
    failed read keeping its reserve;
  - **the cap under load:** with room for exactly two reserves, five
    simultaneous requests (held open by a gated reader) produce two reads and
    three `cap_reached`, and spending ends exactly at $10;
  - **at the limit:** the limit flag is set and the next request is refused
    without reading; another company is unaffected, and the next month starts
    again;
  - **the switch:** turning it off stops new requests but replays still
    answer; the switch records who changed it; invalid values are refused by
    the service and the database check.
- [`tests/m2-security.mjs`](../tests/m2-security.mjs) covers:
  - suggestions refused while off (403);
  - the switch and usage refused when signed out (401), and for a manager, an
    employee, another company's member, another company and a cross-site
    request (403);
  - an invalid value (400);
  - the owner can switch on and read usage;
  - staff see availability without spending.
- **Mutations:** six deliberate code mutations each made a test fail:
  - cap not enforced;
  - switch ignored;
  - failed reads freeing their reserve;
  - limit never shown;
  - staff allowed to switch;
  - the cap never resetting.
- **Local browser check** as the owner, with the fake reader and fictional
  data:
  - the card showed "off" and no panel;
  - **Turn on** showed the panel and "$0.00 of $10.00"; "3 croissants end of
    day" suggested 3 × Croissant;
  - with a fictional $9.99 spend inserted locally, the card showed "$9.99 of
    $10.00" and the panel showed the limit message (the test row was then
    removed);
  - at 390 px wide there was no sideways scrolling and no page errors.
